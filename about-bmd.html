What is BMD?

Buhweju Must Develop – BMD is not a political party. It is not a campaign vehicle. It is not a temporary movement that will disappear after an election.

BMD is a declaration. A declaration by the ordinary people of Buhweju that we have had enough. Enough of being ignored. Enough of being taken for granted. Enough of being given hoes and basins and salt while our children go hungry and our mothers die in childbirth.

BMD is our voice. The voice of the commoner. The voice of the farmer. The voice of the youth with no job. The voice of the mother with no clinic. The voice of the child with no school.

BMD is our answer.

Why BMD Exists

For years, we trusted others. We sent them to Parliament. We gave them our votes. We gave them our hope. We believed they would fight for us. We believed they would remember us. We believed they would bring development.

But they forgot us.

They went to Kampala. They sat in Parliament. They drove in cars. They built houses. They became rich. And they forgot where they came from.

They came back to us only when elections were near. They came with hoes. They came with basins. They came with salt. They came with promises they never kept.

They told us they were working. But we did not see the work.

They told us they were fighting for us. But we did not see the fight.

They told us they had done so much. But we did not see the change.

And we finally lost hope in them.

Our Declaration

We, the ordinary people of Buhweju, have decided. We will no longer send others to speak for us. We will go ourselves.

We will not wait for someone else to solve our problems. We will solve them ourselves.

We will not wait for someone else to build our roads. We will build them ourselves.

We will not wait for someone else to educate our children. We will educate them ourselves.

We are not asking for handouts. We are asking for a chance. A chance to lead. A chance to serve. A chance to change our own lives.

We are not looking for a savior. We are looking for each other. We are looking for ordinary people who are willing to stand up, speak out, and fight for a better Buhweju.

We are looking for ourselves.

What We Believe
Ordinary people know their problems better than any outsider. We have lived here. We have walked these roads. We have missed these meals. We have felt this pain. We need someone to listen, to act, and to stand with us.
Leadership is not about money. It is about the weight of the pain you are fighting for. We have seen leaders who came with money. They built things. They gave things. But they did not build a future. They did not give hope. They did not change our lives.
A poor person with a vision is more powerful than a rich person with a title. A commoner with courage can do what the wealthy with comfort cannot.
The future of Buhweju belongs to us. Not to outsiders. Not to hired workers. Not to those who come only when elections are near. The future belongs to those who live here, who suffer here, who dream here. We are those people. And we are ready.
Our Mission

To unite the ordinary people of Buhweju. To organize our voices. To build our power. To take our rightful place in the decisions that affect our lives.

We will not wait for permission.

We will not wait for someone else to act.

We will not wait for the right moment. The right moment is now.

We will build schools. We will build roads. We will build clinics. We will build systems that work for ordinary people. We will build a Buhweju that works for everyone.

Not through handouts. Through systems.

Not through promises. Through action.

Not through waiting. Through leading.

Our Promise

We promise to never forget where we came from. We promise to always listen to the people. We promise to always fight for the commoner. We promise to never become like those we are replacing.

We promise to leave the door open. So that every ordinary person who comes after us can also walk through.

We promise to build a Buhweju that ordinary people can be proud of.

Our Call

BMD is not just for me. It is for you. It is for all of us.

If you are tired of being ignored, join us.
If you are tired of being taken for granted, join us.
If you are tired of watching your children suffer while leaders eat, join us.
If you believe in a better Buhweju, join us.

Together, we will not fail. Together, we will not retreat. Together, we will not stop until Buhweju is free.

Our Voice

We are ordinary people. We are not rich. We are not powerful. We are not connected.

But we are many. We are united. We are determined. And we are ready.

We are Buhweju Must Develop. And we are coming.
